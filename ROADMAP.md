# Learning Roadmap

A 12-week core path, then an ongoing phase for building a design career. Each phase has one main lesson and one exercise set. Move on when you can pass the checklist, not when the calendar says so.

## Phase 1 · Weeks 1–2 · Pixels and silhouettes

- Pixel-perfect lines and curves
- Canvas sizes and why they matter
- The squint test: a readable silhouette matters more than detail
- Outline styles: black, colored, and none

**Lesson:** [01 · Pixel basics](lessons/01-pixel-basics.md)
**Exercises:** Weeks 1–2 in [exercises/](exercises/README.md)

## Phase 2 · Weeks 3–4 · Color and palettes

- Limited palettes (4 to 16 colors)
- Building a color ramp for each hue
- Hue shifting: shadows drift cool, highlights drift warm
- Keeping saturation under control

**Lesson:** [02 · Palettes and color](lessons/02-palettes-and-color.md)
**Palettes:** [palettes/](palettes/)

## Phase 3 · Weeks 5–6 · Shading and form

- Pick one light direction and keep it
- Core shadow, reflected light, and rim light
- Clusters: group pixels; avoid stray single pixels (noise)
- Dithering: use it deliberately, sparingly
- Hand-placed anti-aliasing

**Lesson:** [03 · Shading and form](lessons/03-shading-and-form.md)

## Phase 4 · Weeks 7–8 · Animation

- Idle loop: 4 frames
- Walk cycle: 6 to 8 frames
- Onion skinning and timing
- Squash and stretch at pixel scale
- Keeping the pixel grid stable from frame to frame

**Lesson:** 04 · Animation (to write when you reach this phase)

## Phase 5 · Weeks 9–10 · Environments and tiles

- Tileable textures
- Building a tileset on a fixed grid (16 px or 32 px)
- Depth through value, not detail
- Simple parallax layers

**Lesson:** 05 · Tiles and environments (to write when you reach this phase)

## Phase 6 · Weeks 11–12 · Game-ready assets

- Sprite sheets with consistent frame sizes and padding
- Icons at 16 px and 32 px
- UI panels and buttons with 9-slice borders
- A one-page style guide for a small project

**Lesson:** 06 · Game-ready assets (to write when you reach this phase)

## Phase 7 · Ongoing · Toward a design career

- **Style guide:** write down your palette, outline rule, light direction, and canvas sizes. Real studios work this way.
- **Portfolio:** 5 to 8 finished pieces, each with process notes: sketch, palette, final, and animation where relevant.
- **Feedback:** share work in art communities and ask specific questions, such as "Does the silhouette read at 1×?"
- **Projects:** finish small things. Game jams are good practice for deadlines and scope.
- **Specialize:** characters, environments, UI, or icons. Choose one direction to lead your portfolio.

## Milestones

- [ ] A readable 16×16 character, outline and all, passes the squint test
- [ ] A 4-frame idle loop that plays without a visible jump
- [ ] A tileset that tiles seamlessly in a 5×5 test grid
- [ ] A sprite sheet and UI panel exported with consistent padding
- [ ] A written style guide for one project
- [ ] A portfolio of 5 finished pieces with process notes
